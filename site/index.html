Plan with the details in view

Pick a mission that fits your flight.

01

Compare the payoff

Sort by advertised payout, credits per hour, duration and distance from your Career airport. Combine filters to narrow the list to the flights that suit your session.

02

Find your approach

Filter destination airports for ILS, RNAV (LPV and LNAV/VNAV), LOC or VOR. Inspect runway lengths, elevations and approach information before choosing a mission.

03

See the route

Preview departure and destination airports on a map. Pin a route while you compare missions, or explore up to three suggested flights ranked by credits per hour.

Airport capability does not guarantee the mission’s assigned approach or aircraft suitability. Choose and launch missions in MSFS.

Optional · Experimental

A hand with the radio.

Radio assist uses the simulator’s ATC menu to send supported responses and change COM frequencies. It works without a microphone or cloud service, with template matching for 28 locales.

It starts off each launch and switches off at landing clearance. It does not fly the aircraft or validate clearances. Taxi, unsupported calls and the landing-clearance readback remain yours; not every locale or runtime wording has been verified in flight.

Ready for your next mission?

Get set up. Then take your pick.

  1. Download your build

    Get a Windows installer or portable ZIP, or the Linux ZIP, plus SHA256SUMS from GitHub Releases. Verify your download before running it. Windows packages are unsigned.

  2. Check the connection

    The local Coherent inspector must expose mainUi and Electronic Flight Bag at http://127.0.0.1:19999/pagelist.json. Setup varies; the companion does not enable it automatically.

  3. Scan Career missions

    Start MSFS, enter Career outside briefing, and press G with the simulator focused to expand its Missions panel. Keep it open and pan the Career map to collect missions.
